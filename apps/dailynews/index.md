## DailyNews

DailyNews is an Android app that brings the day's news from trusted Indian and international sources into one quick, swipeable feed. Each story appears once, with its image, headline and a short summary, even when several outlets report it. When you want the full story, open the original article in the app.

### Features

#### Swipeable News Feed

- Read one story per card, with its image, headline and a short summary
- Browse by category, and pull down to refresh for the latest stories

#### Search, Bookmarks and History

- Search recent news across all sources
- Bookmark stories to read later, and look back at what you've read

#### Comfortable Reading

- Choose a light or dark theme, or follow your phone's setting
- Make the reading text smaller or larger
