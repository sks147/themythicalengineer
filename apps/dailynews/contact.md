DailyNews is made by The Mythical Engineer. To reach us, send an email.

## Email

[themythicalengineer@gmail.com](mailto:themythicalengineer@gmail.com)

Write to us about:

- **Questions, feedback or a problem with the app.** Please include the app version, which you can find in Settings → About.
- **A mistake in a story**, such as a wrong date, summary or source.
- **Publishers** asking us to correct or remove a story. Please include the story's link.
- **Privacy.** Our [Privacy Policy](/apps/dailynews/privacy-policy/) explains how the app handles data.

## Where the Stories Come From

DailyNews is a news aggregator: it brings together stories from Indian and international news publishers. Every story names its publisher, and its author when the publisher gives one. It shows when the story was published and links to the original article. Stories are never more than 30 days old. Headlines, summaries and images belong to their publishers.
